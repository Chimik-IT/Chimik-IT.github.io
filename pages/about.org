#+TITLE: About
#+AUTHOR: Tobias Yang

15 years running Linux in production. Since 2022 also building with it —
RAG pipelines, LLM integration, AI tooling. Bridging Ops reliability and
AI capability: platform infrastructure (RHEL, Ansible, containers) on one
side, RAG systems and LLM-based tooling in Python on the other.

Full implementation: [[https://github.com/youngtree1/RAG-for-LLM4Tumor][RAG-for-LLM4Tumor]] — complete code and architecture
for a RAG pipeline used in soft tissue sarcoma diagnostics. Repo is owned
by the research team (youngtree1); I wrote the entire codebase.

Common Lisper by conviction.
